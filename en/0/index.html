Talks

Four talks from 23 October. Each with video and chapters.

01

19:05 · CZ

Common facility robot issues

Tomáš KonečnýRobotservice

Five minutes from the field: Tomáš Konečný of Robotservice, which services cleaning and other service robots, on what really breaks in operation: unprepared sites, staff who skip daily maintenance, extreme cases (a robot washed with a hose) and a plea to designers to keep wear parts accessible. Most of the video is audience Q&A: sensors and stuck robots, autonomy and central control, outdoor use, theft and vandalism, warranty service. Talk in Czech; part of the Q&A was not recorded.

Chapters

02

19:15 · CZ · Live demo

Physical AI and humanoids in industry

Lukáš VítekIntegra Robotics

Lukáš Vítek of Prague startup Integra Robotics explains physical AI and vision-language-action (VLA) models, why data collection is the bottleneck, and where today's models still fall short for industry: generalization, physical contact, recovering from errors and reliability. He covers where humanoids already work in factories, why Integra combines VLA with classical control and why it recommends wheeled or stationary humanoids over legged ones for industry. Followed by audience Q&A. Talk in Czech.

Chapters

03

20:30 · EN · Live demo

Reachy Mini: open-source desk robot

Fabien DanieauPollen Robotics / Hugging Face

Fabien Danieau of Pollen Robotics (now part of Hugging Face) presents Reachy Mini, the open-source desktop robot: how a Stewart-platform neck and two antennas make it expressive, what's inside (camera, four-microphone array, Raspberry Pi), the software stack and SDKs for Python and JavaScript, and the conversation app that links speech, a language model and the robot's tools. He shows what people build with the 11,000 robots shipped so far and ends with a live demo.

Chapters

04

20:50 · CZ

KORD: Lessons Learned

Ondřej BrunaCo-author and developer of KORD

Ondřej Bruna, co-author and developer of KORD, looks back at building the interface that lets customers drive a 7-axis collaborative robot directly over Ethernet, without the teach pendant. Why ROS wasn't enough, what happened when the team exposed too broad an API, and what the first customer deployment taught them. Three lessons for any robotics software team: documentation with examples, code reviews that actually check, and automated testing. Followed by audience Q&A. Talk in Czech.
