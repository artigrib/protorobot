Note for Prague

OP TAK calls for SMEs (vouchers, Aplikace, Expanze loans) require the project to be carried out outside Prague. What counts is where the project happens, not where the company is registered. For projects in Prague the main options are TA ČR, CzechInvest, EU programmes, accelerators and credits.

Terms and dates change. Always check the programme's website before applying. This is not legal or financial advice.
